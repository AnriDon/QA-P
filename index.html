Ukraine has many charitable foundations.

Sternenko Fund

Contacts of the charitable foundation Contact Sternenko Fund

Why is this important?

  • DRONES INCREASE SURVIVABILITY - Drones provide critical aerial support, enhancing situational awareness and reducing casualties among our forces.
  • DRONES SAVE LIVES - Drones help spot enemy positions, deliver medical supplies, and provide real-time reconnaissance, significantly reducing risks for our troops.
  • DRONES ARE COST-EFFECTIVE - Compared to traditional military equipment, drones are more affordable and easier to maintain.
